Sessions

I offer support in a range of formats, including one-to-one sessions, regular group meetings and educational programmes.

Below, you can find examples of some of the support packages that have worked well for other clients. Each package can be tailored to your individual needs, goals and circumstances.

Please book a discovery call with me so we can discuss the type of support that may be most helpful for you.

Wellbeing Coaching

Personalised sessions designed to support your wellbeing and management of symptoms, using practical, sustainable tools from mindfulness, CBT, and polyvagal-informed approaches to help you build safety, balance, and inner resources in everyday life.

Hypnotherapy

Subconscious-focused work (similar to relaxation) to reframe unhelpful patterns and support long-standing conditions and associated symptoms such as anxiety and low mood. Sessions use personalised scripts tailored to what helps you feel calm, safe, and supported.

FND Support

Specialist guidance for people living with Functional Neurological Disorder. Support focused on understanding your symptoms, building confidence, and reclaiming a sense of control in your life.
