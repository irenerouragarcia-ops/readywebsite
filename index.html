FND Care Guide

Specialist support for FND

I'm Irene Roura García, a nurse specialised in mental health, a wellbeing coach, a hypnotherapist and an FND specialist. I spent four years on an NHS inpatient neuropsychiatry ward alongside people living with Functional Neurological Disorder, and I have worked with the condition ever since.

I work online helping people living with FND regain control over their lives and reduce their symptoms, in English and in Spanish, one to one, in groups, and through courses.

Sessions. One-to-one coaching, psychoeducation, hypnotherapy and specialised FND support, plus group sessions and courses. See sessions and prices.
